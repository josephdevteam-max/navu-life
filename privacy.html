Privacy

Last updated August 27, 2026.

Who we are

NAVU is an independent companion that talks first, then helps you eat. It is not a doctor, not medical advice, and not affiliated with or endorsed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs.

What we send to reply

When you chat, the text of that conversation is sent to NAVU’s backend so we can write a reply and, when you’re ready, suggest a plate. We need that text to answer you. We do not put ads in the chat, and we do not sell your data.

Optional pantry photo

If you snap a shelf, that photo is sent to the backend so we can mark what you already have. You do not have to use the camera. Grocery is Have vs Need for the current plate.

What stays on your device

Settings such as your name, vibe (Lowkey / Everyday / Locked In), food cultures you pick, and cooking method are stored on the device. We do not assume your cultural identity. Mix is the default until you choose otherwise.

Where it runs

The backend runs on Google Firebase in us-central1. We use Firebase App Check so random callers cannot burn the AI from outside the NAVU app.

We don’t sell data

We do not sell your chat, photos, or settings. We do not run a paywall. Free forever means free — not a trial.

Not medical

NAVU helps you talk and eat. It is not a therapist, not a clinician, and not a crisis service. Food suggestions are not diet or medical treatment.

If you need help right now

Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). If you are in immediate danger, call 911. More at 988lifeline.org.

Contact

Questions about this policy: hello@navu.life.
