Privacy

Effective August 30, 2026.

The short version

NAVU uses the information you choose to share to hold the conversation, suggest food, and improve recommendations. We do not sell personal information or place ads in your conversation.

Who we are

NAVU is an independent companion that talks first, then helps you eat. This policy explains what information the app uses, what stays on your device, and the choices available to you.

Information processed when you use NAVU

To answer a message, NAVU may use what you share in the conversation along with recent context, your selected vibe, food preferences, cooking method, optional stove type, and pantry ingredients. A random identifier created by the app may be included so the experience can work without requiring an account.

Optional pantry photo

If you choose to use a pantry photo, NAVU securely processes the image to identify likely food ingredients. The camera is optional. NAVU does not intentionally add the original photo to your profile or grocery history. The detected ingredient names are what the app uses.

What stays on your device

Your conversation, preferred name, appearance setting, vibe, food cultures, dietary and allergy preferences, cooking method, optional stove type, grocery and pantry lists, saved recipes, favorites, and recent meal history are stored locally on your device. NAVU does not require an account. These saved items do not automatically sync between devices or between a browser and the mobile app.

My meals keeps the recipe information needed to reopen and cook a meal. It does not copy the private conversation into your recipe library. Starting a fresh conversation does not delete saved meals, favorites, or the shopping list.

What NAVU may store

NAVU may remember actions you take on meal suggestions, such as saving, cooking, skipping, liking, or asking for something easier. This helps improve future recommendations without requiring your real-world identity. NAVU may also keep general information about which suggestions are useful.

Service providers

NAVU uses trusted service providers to operate the app and provide the features you choose to use. Information is shared only as needed to provide those features. We use encrypted connections and access controls designed to protect information while it is processed.

How information is used

  • Provide companion replies, meal suggestions, pantry recognition, grocery help, and cook-together guidance.
  • Remember recent context and reduce repetitive meal suggestions.
  • Learn which meals are useful from voluntary in-app actions and feedback.
  • Protect, debug, and improve the reliability and safety of the service.

Business model and advertising

NAVU does not sell personal information, charge for app features, or place third-party ads in your conversation. NAVU may use clearly labeled business relationships outside the conversation to support the free service. If you choose to visit another service, that service may process the visit or purchase under its own privacy policy, and NAVU may receive revenue. Revenue does not influence NAVU’s recommendations.

Retention and choices

You can remove individual recipes from My meals. Recent keeps up to 30 unsaved recipes; saved meals and favorites are kept until you remove them or clear local data. Clear my NAVU data in Settings removes the local conversation, kitchen and shopping lists, saved recipes, favorites, meal history, name, and preferences. Clearing browser or app storage, or uninstalling the app, can also remove locally saved information.

Some non-account activity may be kept as needed to operate, protect, and improve NAVU. For a privacy or deletion request, contact us at the address below.

Health and safety

NAVU is not a doctor, therapist, clinician, or crisis service. Its conversation and food suggestions are not medical care, diagnosis, or treatment.

If you need help right now

Call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). If you are in immediate danger, call 911. More at 988lifeline.org.
